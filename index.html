INSIDE ARCHES

A landscape of stone and light

Arches National Park is known for its vivid sandstone formations: natural arches, narrow fins, pinnacles, and balanced rocks shaped over time by erosion. The scenic drive connects major viewpoints and trailheads, making it easy to choose short stops or longer walks. Our photographs capture a few of the park’s striking forms and changing desert light.

Explore the official Arches guide ↗

YOUR BASE IN MOAB

Small town, big red-rock country

Moab is the closest town to Arches, about five miles south of the park entrance, and a convenient base for lodging, meals, supplies, and regional day trips. From town, visitors can plan time in Arches and nearby Canyonlands, then return to Moab for the evening. Most park visits are by car; check current access, timed-entry details, and conditions before setting out.
