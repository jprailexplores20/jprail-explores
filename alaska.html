PRINCE WILLIAM SOUND

Valdez

Valdez pairs a working harbor with a dramatic mountain-and-water setting. From town, the surrounding Prince William Sound opens onto a landscape of deep fjords and tidewater ice; this view captures Columbia Glacier on the water beyond.

PRINCE WILLIAM SOUND

Whittier

Mountain slopes rise directly behind Whittier’s compact waterfront community. The town’s distinctive setting and its road-and-tunnel approach make arrival part of the experience; check current tunnel schedules before traveling.

KENAI PENINSULA · KENAI FJORDS

Seward & nearby glaciers

Seward is a coastal base for exploring the Kenai Peninsula and nearby fjords. Exit Glacier offers a land-based view of ice and mountain terrain, while boat journeys reveal glacier faces meeting the sea. Conditions and access can vary by season.

ALASKA RANGE · MOUNTAIN COUNTRY

Talkeetna

Talkeetna’s welcoming downtown offers a relaxed small-town pause amid a journey through Alaska’s mountain country. On clear days, the surrounding region opens toward broad Alaska Range views; weather determines how much of the peaks can be seen.

FAIRBANKS · DALTON HIGHWAY

North from Fairbanks

Fairbanks is a practical starting point for a northbound drive on the Dalton Highway. The route passes the Trans-Alaska Pipeline and continues into remote Arctic landscapes. Plan carefully for distance, fuel, changing weather, road conditions, and limited services before setting out.
